## Implementation of Android 14 Features on the Google Pixel 3

The main goal is to "enhance functionality and user experience beyond the limitations of the standard Android 12 version", the best on the Pixel 13. Thus, we can "extend unofficial support to the device"

For that, the study build a **custom ROM**. This is an alternative versions of the Android operating system. "Research indicates they offer better efficiency, enhanced security, and greater personalisation than stock ROMs, mainly by eliminating bloatware and introducing optimisation".

The custom ROM in Android 14 add lots of new fonctionnality and extend the life of the phone, the custom ROM is less batery efficient, boot up slower and is less CPU efficient. The only advantage is on the GPU calculation.

**LineageOS** appear to be a good alternative for a easy and efficient custom ROM, may be useful for our project.
