# Challenging Planned Obsolescence: The Windows 10 End-of-Life Crisis and the Case for Linux-Based Hardware Repurposing as a Sustainable E-Waste Mitigation Strategy

> **Source** : Research paper exploring the lifespan of PCs, the global e-waste context, and Linux-based repurposing as a mitigation strategy.

---

## Overview

This paper explores three interconnected topics:

- The **lifespan of PCs** and the phenomenon of planned obsolescence;
- The **global context of e-waste** and its environmental impact;
- **Linux-based hardware repurposing** as a sustainable alternative to device disposal.

---

## Chapter Notes

### The Case for Repurposing Older Computers with Linux

The author surveys various **lightweight Linux distributions** that enable older hardware to remain functional and useful. As an example:

> *"Linux Lite 7.0 idles at just 400–600 MB of RAM and can run on systems with as little as 768 MB RAM and 1 GHz processors."*

This makes it viable for deployment on machines that can no longer run modern versions of Windows.
