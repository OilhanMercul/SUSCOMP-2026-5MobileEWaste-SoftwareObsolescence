# Why Good Product Go Bad

In this article the author explain the differents mecanism that explains why products that are initially good can become bad over time and how to counter that. From an indivodual point of view he gives a checklist to do before buying a product with some thing like prefer local control or open standards.

It generaly explain how vendors try to harvest us and why they do that.

The article is not very specific on the software alternatives that we can use to counter the planned obsolescence but it is a good overview of why and how the obsolescence is planned and how it can be counter at a more general level.
