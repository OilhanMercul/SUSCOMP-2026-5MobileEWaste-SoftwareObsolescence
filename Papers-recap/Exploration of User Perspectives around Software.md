## Exploration of User Perspectives around Software Data-Related Challenges Associated with IoT Repair and Maintenance against Obsolescence

Not only about laptop/smartphone but all IoT devices, more large spectrum


### Facts

+ "Despite their benefits, IoT devices are still being designed, manufactured, and disposed irresponsibly". For exemple "monopolistic practices such as serialisation and part-pairing" make the life harder for third-party repair + lack of basic repair and technical skills and unavailability of accessible and timely repair information.
+ "E-waste is the fastest growing waste stream globally and it has been increasing at the rate of 3-5% annually in Europe"
+ Social perspective is also important : West countries send their waste to developing countries (India, Bangladesh, Congo) affecting entire villages, young children and women especially
+ Microsoft’s decision to discontinue Windows 10 OS could push over 240 million personal computers straight to landfills.


### Actual Solutions

+ France repairability score
+ rise of repair cafe
+ methodologie of circular economy


### Software focus

Definition : "Software repair was seen as the practice of updating older smartphones, which were no longer supported by the manufacturers, to their latest versions."

Interesting and new concept here : **"Users see ownership as shared between themselves and manufacturers, with control over hardware but reliance on manufacturers for software updates due to lack of expertise and security concerns**" 
You don't actually control the software, you trust (or need to trust) the manufacturer.

**Open Source** can be a solution here but "Other participants were worried about losing manufacturer support privileges if they tinkered with the software". Furthermore, it appears more vulnerable to **cyberattacks**. Same for non-official communities : "Participants rejected support offered by post-official communities due to security and accountability concerns"

You don't have power on the **agreement** signed with the manufacturer : there is too much information at one time and it's design like an "all-or-nothing" agreement

For that consumer also need **transparency and high level of trust** in the company. "In the absence of trust, participants said that they would stop usingthe device, discard it or replace it with a device from a more trustedbrand when faced with an adverse scenario"
