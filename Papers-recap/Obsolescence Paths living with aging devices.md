## Obsolescence Path living with aging devices

Disposable tech : "devices not meant to be reparable or recyclable but rather replaced". Smartphone is the best exemple.

The obsolescence experience is "not only conditioned by hardware and device factors (broken screen or worn batteries), but also by software-related factors such as slowness, storage saturation and much more" => The goal is to find the more impactful factors and how they are linked.


Software obsolescence : 20% of smartphone replacement in France. 
+ Rapid hardware and sotware evolution 
=> Android (75% to 80% of the market): new major version every 6 month
=> Apple (20 to 25%) : new version every year.

+ Duopoly 

=> No standardisation

+ Furthermore, the design choice and support decision due to the market logic influence our practice and the replacement rate of our phones.

3 main obsolescence factors : 
+ Storage saturation
+ Update issues. With time, update can be unvailable or take too much space. It led to inability to use app. Sometimes, it is the update that can cause critical problem

obsolescence path : "obsolescence should rather be considered an incremental process"
+ The software and hardware issues can be entangled, so it is difficult to identify the origin of the encountered malfunction, and hence to tackle it
+ The need to chance device can come from the use you make of it in your social or work life


Multiple strategie to enhance the life of your phone : Repair and maintenance, extension, giving up foncionnality.
+ The one that can be useful for us : Bypassing
"Bypassing strategies consist in circumventing issues, to enable normally unusable functions"
For exemple, using a different OS System for upgrading the OS, or change the source code for using different piece of hardware is one doesn't work : "Theo directly modified the source code of his smartphone to be able to unlock it with the volume buttons instead of his broken power button"
